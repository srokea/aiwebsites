**💰 How Pool Campaigns Work**

Pool campaigns don't use a fixed CPM. The campaign has a **set budget (the pool)**, and every clipper gets a cut based on **their share of the total qualified views**.

**The math**

If your approved clips bring in **10% of all counted views** in a **\$10K pool**, you earn **\$1,000** (before fees).

* There's **no fixed rate per 1K views**. Your payout depends on how you perform compared to everyone else in the pool.  
* There's **no individual payout cap**. If your clips pull the most views, you take the biggest share.  
* Your effective CPM moves as the campaign goes on. The more total views the pool gets, the less each view is worth, so **your share of views matters more than your raw view count.**

**Track it live**

You can watch your **share of the pool and estimated earnings** update in real time on your dashboard. These are estimates until the pool closes, so your numbers will shift as other clippers post.

**When the pool ends**

1. All pending clips are reviewed.  
2. The pool is closed and final payouts are locked in.  
3. Payouts go through the standard **3-day hold** before they're released.

**Tips to maximise your cut**

* **Post early.** The sooner your clips go up, the longer they have to build views.  
* **Volume and quality both count.** More strong clips means a bigger share.  
* **Only approved views count.** Follow the campaign brief so your clips get approved.
