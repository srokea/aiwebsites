## Pricing Ideas

### Basic Website

Designed for businesses **without Booksy**.

Features:
- no online booking
- contact information
- price list
- reviews
- photos

Pricing:
- Upfront: approximately **400–500 PLN**
- Subscription: approximately **150–200 PLN/month**

Features:
