# Apple-Inspired Storytelling & Typography

## Philosophy

Do not think in terms of "sections".

Think in terms of scenes.

Each screen should communicate one clear idea before introducing the next.

Users should never feel overwhelmed by information.

---

## Typography

Typography is the main design element.

Prefer:

* very large headlines
* short sentences
* generous whitespace
* strong hierarchy
* high contrast

Avoid:

* long paragraphs
* multiple messages competing for attention
* unnecessary labels

Whenever possible, communicate one idea with one sentence.

---

## Storytelling

Each scroll should answer one question and naturally create curiosity for the next one.

Example:

Question

↓

Answer

↓

New question

↓

Answer

↓

Call to action

Never reveal everything at once.

Build anticipation.

---

## Scene Transitions

When moving between scenes:

* the current content should gracefully fade away
* allow a brief moment of visual silence
* reveal the next scene naturally

Transitions should feel cinematic.

Avoid instant replacements.

---

## Text Animation

Headlines should feel alive.

Preferred effects:

* fade up
* blur reveal
* gradual opacity
* subtle character or word stagger
* gentle movement (10–20px)

Animations should never be dramatic.

The goal is elegance, not attention seeking.

---

## Focus

At any moment the user should know exactly where to look.

If multiple elements are present:

* one element should dominate
* secondary content should remain visually quiet

Never compete for attention.

---

## Scroll Rhythm

Alternate between moments of energy and moments of calm.

Example:

Large headline

↓

Whitespace

↓

Beautiful image

↓

Whitespace

↓

Single sentence

↓

Interactive visual

↓

Whitespace

This rhythm makes the page feel premium.

---

## Premium Feeling

Design every screen as if it were a keynote presentation slide.

Every scene should be beautiful enough to stand on its own.

The user should naturally want to continue scrolling.

---

## Emotional Goal

The user should feel:

"I'm curious."

↓

"This looks polished."

↓

"They clearly care about details."

↓

"I trust these people."

The website should earn trust through craftsmanship rather than marketing claims.
