# AI Web Agency — Operating Context (June 2026)

## 1. Mission

Build a small, repeatable system for acquiring and serving local service businesses with subscription-based websites.

Primary objective is NOT one-off website delivery, but:

- predictable lead acquisition
- consistent sales conversion via cold outreach
- recurring monthly revenue (MRR)
- scalable production of websites using AI tools

This is an execution-first business. Optimization comes after validation.

---

## 2. Business Model

Simple, single-offer structure:

- 500 PLN one-time setup fee
- 200 PLN monthly subscription

Includes:
- website creation
- hosting
- maintenance
- minor updates

Important constraint:
→ no multiple pricing tiers
→ no complex packages
→ one clear offer only

---

## 3. Target Market Strategy

Focus on one niche at a time (sequential scaling).

Priority order:

1. barbers / hair salons
2. beauty salons
3. massage services
4. dieticians
5. personal trainers
6. tutors
7. event services
8. car detailing

Initial execution must focus ONLY on first niche until traction is proven.

---

## 4. Lead Generation System

Primary source: Google Maps scraping (manual at start).

All leads stored in Google Sheets.

Sheet schema:

- business name
- phone number
- city
- website URL
- Facebook
- Instagram
- Booksy presence
- number of reviews
- business quality score (1–5)
- contact status
- notes

Goal:
→ build structured outbound database before scaling outreach

---

## 5. CRM / Tracking System

Two tools are used:

- Google Sheets → structured lead database (source of truth)
- Notion → cold call scripts + conversation framework + objection handling

Notion contains:
- cold call flow
- hook variations
- objection responses
- demo structure

Sheets = data layer  
Notion = execution layer

---

## 6. Cold Calling System

Primary objective of cold call:

→ NOT selling
→ ONLY booking a short online demo

Call structure:

1. confirm decision-maker (owner / responsible person)
2. hook
3. short pitch
4. objection handling
5. booking demo

Critical rule:
→ call success = scheduled demo, not sale

---

## 7. Hook Strategy

Two scenarios:

### Scenario A: No website exists
- mention difficulty finding online presence or pricing information
- use this as entry point into conversation

### Scenario B: Social media / Booksy exists
- acknowledge existing presence positively
- position website as upgrade, not replacement

Important:
→ never attack current online presence
→ always position as enhancement

---

## 8. Pitch Style

Tone:
- informal
- human-to-human (H2H)
- non-corporate
- local operator identity (student + local builder)

Core message:
- building websites for local businesses in the area
- simple, modern websites
- focus on improving visibility and trust

No marketing language.
No agency framing.

---

## 9. Demo Structure

If interest is confirmed:

- 10–15 minute online call
- focus is presentation of prepared website concept
- NOT a sales presentation

Important framing:
→ “short walkthrough of concept I prepared for you”

Before meeting:
- send simple link to concept
- no technical explanations (hosting, stack, etc.)

---

## 10. SMS Follow-up Logic

If call not answered:

- wait ~30 minutes
- send SMS only after delay

SMS purpose:
→ generate reply / re-engagement
→ NOT to sell directly

---

## 11. Link Requests Handling

If client asks for website link:

Response strategy:

- clarify it is a prepared concept, not a live production site
- redirect to demo meeting
- avoid technical explanations (hosting, deployment, etc.)

---

## 12. Objection Handling Principles

Common objections:

- “We already use Booksy”
- “Not interested”
- “Need to think about it”
- “How much does it cost?”
- “We are fully booked”

Rules:

- do not argue
- do not push
- ask clarifying questions
- identify real underlying constraint (time, value perception, trust, etc.)

Goal:
→ understand objection, not defeat it

---

## 13. Pricing Policy

If asked:
→ give price immediately

No deflection.
No delayed reveal strategy.

Simplicity > manipulation.

---

## 14. Booksy Positioning

Website is NOT a replacement for Booksy.

Website purpose:

- brand building
- trust improvement
- central information hub
- visibility consolidation

Booksy remains operational tool for booking.

---

## 15. QR System (Future Expansion Layer)

Post-visit QR concept:

Initial idea:
- QR → website

Improved version:
- QR → landing hub with:
  - reviews submission
  - booking redirect (Booksy)
  - social media links
  - pricing visibility

This is a potential future upsell / subscription feature.

---

## 16. Volume Strategy

Outbound targets:

- ~40 cold calls per day
- 3 days per week
- ~160 calls per week
- ~1600–1700 calls over 3 months

Assumption:
→ outbound sales is a volume-driven system

No early abandonment allowed.

---

## 17. Sales Philosophy

Identity positioning:

Not a salesperson.

Positioning:
→ young local builder creating websites for real businesses

Communication principles:
- simple language
- natural flow
- no corporate framing
- conversational tone

This is a competitive advantage vs agencies.

---

## 18. Execution Priority Order

Strict priority hierarchy:

1. build lead database
2. execute cold calls (first 100–200)
3. acquire first paying clients
4. only then optimize:
   - website system
   - automation
   - additional services

No premature optimization allowed.

---

- Claude is primary AI assistant for:
  - website generation
  - design decisions
  - copywriting support
  - iteration and improvement loops

- Google Sheets = lead system
- Notion = sales system
- Claude = production + thinking layer

---

## 20. Non-Negotiable Constraints

- no overengineering
- no complex pricing structures
- no SaaS-style design direction for client websites
- focus on conversion, not aesthetics alone
- execution > planning
